# Hand-off template

After the build finishes, print:

1. A `[View the report](computer://<absolute-path-to-html>)` link.
2. A three-sentence headline, figures only — no recommended actions:
   - **Top KPI sentence** — Blended ROAS for the last 28 days, plus a delta vs the previous period.
   - **Most striking attribution figure** — largest TrackBee-vs-platform count gap (stated neutrally) or highest-ROAS channel.
   - **Lowest funnel step rate** — the lowest step-to-step rate and the lost-shopper count at that step.
3. A one-line note on the controls: "Toggle 3d / 7d / 28d at the top, plus the sankey filters under Customer Journeys."

**Don't paste the tables into chat.** The report is the deliverable.
